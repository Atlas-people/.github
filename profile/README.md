# Atlas People

**Atlas shows leaders whether their organisation can deliver the plan, what could get in the way, and what to address first.**

Atlas People ApS is a Copenhagen software company (CVR 44735520), founded 2024. Atlas combines its own psychometric assessment and survey, organisational context, AI and human judgement to help enterprise leadership teams test how an organisation may respond before a major decision is implemented.

The question Atlas answers: *Can this organisation execute the plan, and what needs to change?*

How it works: **Read → Forecast → Recommend.** Atlas informs the decision. People remain responsible for making it. Atlas does not automate employment decisions or rank people.

## Principles

1. Evidence before opinion.
2. Context before conclusion.
3. Decisions before reports.
4. Learning from outcomes.
5. Dignity throughout.
